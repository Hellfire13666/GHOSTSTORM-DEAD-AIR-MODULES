## ☣️ WHAT THIS SPECIAL MODULE DOES

The geography and location-facing module family. Organizes public OpenStreetMap place data, geographic event references and readable geographic context. Historical development involved Earthwraith, public-history lookups and route adapters.

## 🎛️ RESEARCH INPUTS

Places, cities, category searches, geographic areas, and coordinates supplied by a researcher.

## 🌈 WHAT YOU SEE

Public place results or geospatial observations with names, categories, location context, data-source attribution, and error/timeout information when provided.

## ⚡ EXAMPLE // EXPERIENCE THE WORKFLOW

**Illustrative research example, not an executable command:** Search public libraries around a city → compare returned OpenStreetMap places → examine source attribution and results count → export only approved research notes.

**Example status:** Workflow illustration only. There is no separately verified `ghost-map` terminal command published in this repository.
